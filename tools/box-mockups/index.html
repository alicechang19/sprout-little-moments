Blind box: four directions

Each direction picks its own box shape, print, opening and finish, aiming to make the box feel more collectible, more like the Sprout world, and less plain. All four keep the Sprout leaf-drop, the series name and "6 to collect". Today's box is at the end for comparison.

Direction A

Seed packet

The figure comes in a seed packet. You tear the top to "plant" it, and the back reads like growing notes. Humble and tactile, and very Sprout.

Shape
A flat, crimp-topped paper pouch, about 10 × 15 cm, a little puffy with the figure inside. They stack and fan out like a seed rack.
Print
Two inks on kraft: forest green line drawing plus a leaf-green fill. A hand-set label block ("Variety: Mystery") and a rubber stamp with the series number.
Opening
A tear notch and a perforated line across the top, like a real seed packet. The figure and a folded collector card slide out.
Finish
Uncoated recycled kraft, letterpress-style ink with slight texture, and a crimped heat-seal top.
KraftForest inkLeafStamp

Why it feels collectible: it looks like a seed collection. Each packet is a "variety", and the stamp numbers your find.

Direction B

Flower pot

The box is a little terracotta pot. Pull the paper leaf and the Sprout grows up out of the soil, so the opening is the moment.

Shape
A tapered cylinder with a rolled rim, about 9 cm across. A soil-coloured lid sits inside the rim with a die-cut leaf standing up from it.
Print
Mostly material, very little ink: a cream wraparound label band with the wordmark and the lineup as six drops, plus a debossed Sprout drop on the pot.
Opening
Pull the leaf. The lid lifts on a card riser and the figure rises with it, standing in its pot. The pot stays on as a display stand.
Finish
Matte speckled terracotta board, a flocked soft-touch "soil" lid, and an uncoated cream band.
TerracottaSoilLabelLeaf tab

Why it feels collectible: the packaging becomes the display. Six pots on a shelf make a little garden.

Direction C

Pop cube

An unmistakable blind-box toy: a chunky glossy cube in saturated colour, the lineup in silhouette on every side, and a holographic seal you have to break.

Shape
A squat cube, about 7 cm, with tuck-in top flaps. Every side is a different colour, so a row of boxes reads like a rainbow on a shelf.
Print
Bold flat colour with a chunky rounded wordmark outlined in navy. The six Sprouts appear as silhouettes in the lineup, a holographic "?" drop sits on the front, and a "6 to collect" sticker.
Opening
Peel the holographic seal across the top, open the flaps, and inside is a foil bag with the figure and a collector card.
Finish
Gloss laminate, spot-UV over the silhouettes, a holographic foil seal and drop.
SkyGrassSunHolo foilInk

Why it feels collectible: it borrows the language people already know from blind-box toys: silhouettes, seal, foil and the thrill of the unknown.

Direction D

Greenhouse window

A quiet, premium box with a leaf-shaped window. Through frosted vellum you can see a blurred green shape: enough to tease, never enough to know.

Shape
A slim upright box, about 7 × 12 cm, with a matchbox-style inner drawer. A small green tab sticks out of the side.
Print
Almost blank. Fine green foil line work for the leaf window and veins, a light serif wordmark, and the lineup as six outlined drops on the side.
Opening
Push the tab and the drawer slides out, wrapped in a vellum sleeve. Unwrap it to find out who it is. The window is the reveal.
Finish
Soft-touch white board, green foil, frosted vellum, and a blind-embossed series number.
Soft whiteMistFoilDeep leaf

Why it feels collectible: it's a keepsake. The window makes every box a small mystery you can almost see, and the drawer makes opening slow.

The cream carton

A tall cream carton with a green tear strip, a leaf-drop "?" on the front, and the lineup and moments on the sides.

Keep
The tear strip is a great interaction in 3D. Any direction can keep a version of it (A's perforation, C's seal).
Gap
Little colour or illustration, and nothing that says "toy". It reads more like a tea box than a collectible.
