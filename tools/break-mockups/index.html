Five-minute break: options

Directions for the break before each new box. Each one is frozen at the same moment, a Daydream break with 2:18 left, over the Daydream scene at the app's 1440 × 900. The copy is today's copy.

NOWToday’s card

For comparison: a centred card with the task, a ring timer and a note.

AA window onto the Sprout’s sky

The top of the card is the Sprout's own sky, and the timer is a sun crossing it. Each Sprout gets its own: Little Light's would be the dusk sky with a moon, Rest's a night sky.

  • Ties the break to the Sprout more than anything else here.
  • Needs a small sky drawing for each of the six.

BSide by side, in small steps

A wider card with the timer on the left and the task split into two or three steps people can tick off. Ticking is just for fun; the box still waits for the timer.

  • Easier to follow away from the screen, and ticking feels like doing something.
  • Each task needs rewriting as steps. Too wide for phones, so it stacks there.

CThe whole screen steps away

No card. The scene fades into a soft blur of the Sprout's sky with a big, quiet clock. It reads as "go away from the screen" more than "fill this in".

  • The calmest option, and the most obvious that it's a break.
  • Hides the Sprout completely while you wait.

DWater the leaf

The ring becomes the Sprout leaf, and it fills up as the break goes on, like it's being watered. When it's full, the next box comes out. The card is narrower and centred.

  • The most Sprout-like timer, and it uses the leaf shape that's already on the loading screen.
  • The leaf is less exact than a ring for seeing how much time is left, so the time sits under it.

EA pill in the dock, no dialog

No dialog at all. The break sits where "Open another box" was, so you can keep turning and tapping the Sprout while you wait. Tap the pill to see the full task.

  • Never blocks the Sprout, and there's nothing to close.
  • Easier to ignore, so it's less of a nudge to actually step away.
