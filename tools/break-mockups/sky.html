Options A + C together

The break takes over the whole screen, washed in the Sprout's own sky, and the timer is that sky's sun crossing from one side to the other. It rises when the break starts and sets when it's done, and then the next box comes out. Earlier options are on the options page.

Daydream, from the click to back with your Sprout

"Open another box" brings up the break with the sun on the left horizon and one button, "Start my break". Once it's started, "Close for now" takes you back to the Sprout you just unboxed, and a small timer replaces the "Open another box" button. Tap the timer to bring the break back. When it runs out, the next box comes out wherever you are.

On a phone

The same layout stacked: the arc and clock up top, the task below the horizon. The timer under the Sprout fits the same spot.

Every Sprout gets its own sky

The colours come from each Sprout's scene. Rest gets a moon with stars, and Little Light gets a star on its dusk sky. The rest have a sun, with something small from their own scene: clouds for Daydream, birds for Read, hills for Explore, sparkles for Cheer.
